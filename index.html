01 About

I'm an MSc student in Climate Modeling and Risk Management at the Institute of Water and Flood Management (IWFM), BUET, with hands-on experience in meteorological forecast verification, statistical analysis, and large-scale weather data processing. I'm skilled in Python for meteorological data analysis and visualization, and experienced in evaluating forecast accuracy through hindcasting tropical cyclones and analyzing extreme precipitation events using the WRF and MPAS‑A atmospheric models.

  • Extreme precipitation & heatwave modeling (WRF, MPAS‑A)
  • Tropical cyclone hindcasting & forecast verification
  • Climate risk assessment for coastal & urban Bangladesh
  • Nature-based Solutions for climate-resilient infrastructure
  • Python-based meteorological data analysis & visualization

04 Field Experience

As part of the GCA-funded coastal climate resilience project, I've taken part in field-based climate risk assessments across four climate-vulnerable coastal municipalities in Bangladesh, surveying salinity intrusion, flood exposure, and water scarcity affecting local WASH infrastructure. This work combined site visits and stakeholder engagement with LGED engineers and local officials to ground technical GIS trend analysis in on-the-ground conditions, feeding directly into Nature-based Solutions proposals for climate-resilient water management infrastructure in these towns.
